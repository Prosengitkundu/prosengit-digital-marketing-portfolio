Publishing policy

What makes feedback trustworthy?

Feedback may come from a client, learner or collaborator who has genuinely worked with Prosengit Kundu. Before publication, the wording, name, role, organisation and project context are checked with the person who provided it. Permission is required, and a review can be withdrawn or corrected.

  • Attribution is accurate and not implied when it has not been verified
  • Project scope is described without exposing private client information
  • Results are stated only when evidence and permission exist
  • Concept and demo work are never presented as client case studies
