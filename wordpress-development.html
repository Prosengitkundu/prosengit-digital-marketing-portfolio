What this service is for

The work should answer a business question, not fill a checklist.

WordPress is useful when a business needs a flexible content management system and someone will regularly update the website. I help plan, build, improve or maintain WordPress business websites with a focus on content clarity, useful editing workflows, search fundamentals, performance and sensible plugin decisions.

I first learn who will edit the site, how often content changes and which parts of the current setup are worth keeping. The technical choice should serve the maintenance reality. A simple, well-understood WordPress site is usually more useful than a complex build no one feels safe updating.

In practice

What maintainability looks like in WordPress

A WordPress website is maintained by people, not by a plugin list. Before choosing a theme, builder or plugin, I ask who will edit content, how often changes happen, which integrations are essential and what happens when an update causes a conflict. Those questions are part of the development scope.

The SEO layer should be equally understandable. Important pages need a deliberate URL, title, heading, internal-link and indexation strategy. Multiple plugins should not compete to create metadata or structured data, and a visual builder should not hide the core content from the person responsible for reviewing it.

I also distinguish a one-time cleanup from ongoing care. A performance review, update plan or handover can reduce risk, but a site that needs frequent changes, backups and security monitoring may require a maintenance agreement or an internal owner.

Inputs and review

What I need and how we review it

For WordPress work, I need the current hosting, admin access when appropriate, theme and plugin context, backup status, the people who edit content and the changes that are most urgent. Never send passwords in an initial enquiry; access can be arranged safely later.

Review is based on the agreed pages or templates, editing workflow, SEO settings, mobile experience, performance observations, update safety and the clarity of the handover notes.

Questions clients usually ask

Before you decide, here is the useful detail.

Do you develop WordPress websites from scratch?
Yes, where the project needs a business website, landing page or content structure that can be edited by the owner. The theme, builder and plugin approach is chosen after discussing maintenance needs.
Can you optimise an existing WordPress website?
Yes. I can review technical SEO, metadata, indexation, content structure, images, plugins, speed issues and conversion paths before recommending targeted fixes.
Do you provide WordPress hosting and premium plugins?
Hosting and paid licences are normally owned and paid for by the client. I can recommend what the project needs and configure agreed tools, but third-party costs remain separate.
Is WordPress better than custom HTML?
Neither is universally better. WordPress suits frequent content editing and a CMS workflow; custom HTML can be simpler and lighter for stable content. The right choice depends on who will maintain the site.
