In practice

From a broad question to an actionable roadmap

A consultation is most useful when the question is explicit. “How do I grow?” can become a review of the audience, offer, search demand, current website, acquisition channels and conversion path. “Should I start Google Ads?” can become a comparison of intent, budget, landing-page readiness, tracking and the time available to learn.

Research is documented with assumptions so you can tell what is known, estimated or still needs validation. A competitor page may be visible without being effective; a keyword may have volume without commercial value; a marketplace category may be busy without being a good fit. Those distinctions are more useful than a decorative strategy deck.

The roadmap then assigns an order: what to fix now, what to test next, what the business needs to measure and what can wait. Training and workshops use the same principle, connecting the concept to the team’s own website, accounts and daily decisions.
