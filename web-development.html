In practice

What makes a business website useful

Good web development is not only a visual exercise. It is an information system for people who do not know your business yet. A visitor should understand the offer, recognise whether the service fits, find answers to objections and reach a clear next step without hunting through the navigation.

That means content is considered before the page is polished. I work out which page owns which message, what the main CTA is, what needs proof or explanation and which details belong in a FAQ or service page. Semantic HTML, descriptive links, keyboard focus, labelled forms and useful responsive behaviour then support that structure.

A lightweight build is also easier to hand over. The goal is not to remove every future change; it is to make the current system understandable, fast enough for real users and ready for the next layer of SEO, analytics or campaign work.

Inputs and review

What I need and how we review it

For a website project, I need to know who the site serves, which pages are essential, who supplies copy and images, what platform will host it and who will maintain it after handover. Existing brand files, forms and analytics requirements should be shared early.

Review is based on content clarity, responsive behaviour, keyboard and form usability, page speed fundamentals, metadata, link structure and whether a visitor can reach the agreed next action without confusion.

Questions clients usually ask

Before you decide, here is the useful detail.

Can you build a website using only HTML and CSS?
Yes. Projects can use HTML and CSS with JavaScript where interaction is useful. A custom build is a good fit when the content is relatively stable and performance, control or simplicity matter.
Do you provide website copy and SEO?
I can structure and optimise provided content, research page topics and recommend copy. Full copywriting or extensive content production is scoped separately so the amount of work is clear.
Will you host the website?
The hosting and domain remain in your ownership. I can prepare the files for a static host or work with the hosting environment you choose, subject to access and platform limits.
Can you improve an existing website instead of rebuilding it?
Often. I will review the current site, content, technical constraints and maintenance needs before recommending a rebuild. A focused landing page or template cleanup may be enough.
