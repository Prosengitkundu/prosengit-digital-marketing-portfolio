In practice

Local relevance without location stuffing

Local SEO works when the business information, service offer and customer context agree. A profile category should describe the real business, the website should explain the actual service and the contact path should make it easy for a nearby customer to take the next step. Repeating a city name does not create that agreement.

I look for the questions that local searchers need answered: where the service is available, what is included, whether an appointment is required, how to contact the business and what makes the service appropriate. Those answers can inform a profile, service page, FAQ, photo plan and review request without producing thin city pages.

Measurement should match local behaviour. Calls, messages, direction requests, profile actions, local impressions and form submissions can each tell a different part of the story. The right set depends on the business, its eligibility and the access available.

Inputs and review

What I need and how we review it

For local SEO, I need the genuine business name, eligible address or service area, services, opening/contact information, website access where relevant and a view of how customers currently find or contact the business. I do not need or want fabricated location data.

Review is based on information consistency, profile completeness, service-page usefulness, local intent coverage, legitimate review workflow and the actions that can actually be measured for the business.

Questions clients usually ask

Before you decide, here is the useful detail.

Do you create Google Business Profiles in countries where I do not operate?
No. I can support a genuine business and its real service areas, but I do not create fake locations, virtual offices or profiles to manipulate local results.
Can you help a service-area business?
Yes. The website and profile can be planned around the actual service area, eligible address setup, service categories and the questions customers ask before contacting the business.
Do you write location pages for every city?
Only where each page can provide genuinely useful, unique information. A small set of strong service-area pages is better than dozens of doorway pages.
How long does local SEO take?
Profile and on-site improvements can be delivered on a project timeline; visibility and enquiries depend on competition, proximity, relevance, reviews and ongoing activity.
