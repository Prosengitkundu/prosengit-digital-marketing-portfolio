SEO services

Do you provide SEO services?+

Yes — SEO is my primary specialization. That includes keyword research, SERP analysis, on-page SEO, technical SEO, local SEO, SEO audits, competitor analysis, image SEO, content strategy, Google Search Console and Analytics setup, ongoing monitoring and YouTube SEO.

Do you provide website SEO audits?+

Yes. A full audit covers crawlability, indexation, metadata, heading structure, internal linking, page speed, Core Web Vitals, structured data and content quality — delivered as a prioritized fix list (impact × effort) explained in plain language, so your developer can implement it or I can.

How long does SEO take to show results?+

Honest expectations: technical fixes can show effect within weeks; content-driven gains typically mature over 2–4 months; competitive head terms take longer and depend on the authority you build along the way. Anyone guaranteeing a #1 ranking in 30 days is selling risk, not results — I don't make guarantees I can't control.

Do you do local SEO for Khulna / my city?+

Yes. Local SEO covers Google Business Profile optimization, local keyword targeting, NAP consistency across directories, local citations, location landing pages and review strategy. For local service businesses this is usually the highest-ROI channel because competition is limited to your area.

What do you need from me to start SEO work?+

Access or screenshots to Google Search Console and Analytics (or permission to set them up), your business goals and service areas, and any previous SEO work history. From there, an audit and keyword research define the plan — you approve scope before ongoing work begins.
