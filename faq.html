Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before starting an SEO, advertising, design, website, lead generation, consultation, or training project.

How do I choose the right service?+

Start with the smallest useful step. An SEO Audit can reveal technical and content opportunities, Keyword Research can guide your content, and a consultation can help you choose the right channel. I will recommend a practical path based on your goal, audience, current assets, and budget.

Are the listed prices final?+

No. All prices are starting rates. The final quote may vary depending on the project scope, number of pages, content requirements, account condition, technical complexity, timeline, and client requirements.

Is the Google or Meta advertising budget included?+

No. Advertising spend is paid directly by the client to Google or Meta. The service price covers campaign setup, targeting, creative direction, tracking, or management as described in the selected service or package.

How long does a project take?+

Small services can be delivered in one or two days. A complete SEO package may take 15–30 days, while websites, ongoing social media, and custom training follow a timeline agreed after reviewing the scope.

Do you work with clients outside Bangladesh?+

Yes. Communication, consultation, design, SEO, advertising setup, and training can be delivered remotely for clients in different countries and time zones.

Do you offer one-to-one consultation?+

Yes. One-to-one digital marketing, SEO strategy, Google Ads, Meta Ads, and business growth sessions are available by appointment. The goal is to leave you with clear decisions and an actionable next step.

Do you provide revisions?+

Revision terms depend on the selected service or package and are agreed before work begins. Simple design services and packages include a defined revision allowance; larger custom projects receive a written scope.

Do you offer training for teams and institutions?+

Yes. Digital marketing, graphic design, and database management training can be customized for individual learners, teams, educational institutions, and development programs.
