Services and scope

What can you hire me to do?

What services do you provide?
The main services are SEO and website optimisation, Meta Ads, Google and YouTube Ads, custom web development, WordPress development, local SEO, B2B lead generation and digital marketing consulting, research and training. Related design and social media support can be scoped when it serves the wider project.
Can I hire you for one focused task?
Yes. A technical audit, landing page, keyword map, campaign review or prospect research batch can be a useful starting point. A larger ongoing engagement is not required unless the work itself needs continuity.
Do you work with international clients?
Yes. I work remotely with businesses in Bangladesh and other markets including the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, Australia, Europe, the Middle East and Singapore. I do not create false offices or location claims.
What information should I provide before getting a quote?
Share your website or business link, what you sell, who you want to reach, the market or country, the problem you want to solve, any deadline and what you have tried already. This is enough to decide what should be reviewed next.
SEO and search visibility

Questions about organic growth.

How long does SEO take?
SEO has no fixed result date. Technical and on-page improvements can be completed on a project timeline, but visibility and qualified enquiries depend on competition, site history, content, technical health, authority and demand. I explain the sequence without promising rankings, traffic or leads.
How much do SEO services cost?
Starting points are listed on the pricing page, but a final quote depends on the number of pages, research depth, access, implementation responsibility and ongoing cadence. A smaller audit or keyword project may be more appropriate than a monthly package.
Do you provide international SEO?
Yes. International SEO can include market-aware research, information architecture, page and language considerations, internal links, content mapping and hreflang recommendations when genuinely needed. I avoid duplicate country pages and unnatural location repetition.
Can you optimise an existing website?
Yes. I can audit the existing pages and recommend what to improve, consolidate, rewrite or leave alone. A full rebuild is not automatically required.
Do you guarantee first-page rankings?
No. Search engines, competition and market demand are outside any freelancer’s complete control. I provide a method, clear deliverables and measurement recommendations instead of a guarantee.
Google, YouTube and Meta Ads

Questions about paid acquisition.

Do you manage Google Ads and Meta Ads?
Yes. Campaign strategy, account setup, audience or keyword research, creative direction, tracking, optimisation and reporting can be scoped for Google, YouTube, Facebook and Instagram depending on the project.
Is ad spend included in your fee?
No. Your advertising budget is paid directly to Google or Meta and stays separate from the service fee. The budget recommendation depends on the offer, market, conversion path and learning goal.
Can you improve the quality of leads?
I can review targeting, offer, form questions, landing page, creative and follow-up to improve the chances of a relevant enquiry. I cannot guarantee lead volume or quality because those also depend on the market and sales process.
Websites and WordPress

Questions about building the digital foundation.

Can you build HTML, CSS and JavaScript websites?
Yes. Custom-coded business sites and landing pages can be built with responsive, semantic structure, useful interactions, metadata, accessible forms, image optimisation and a clear handover.
Do you develop WordPress websites?
Yes. WordPress builds, existing-site improvements, WordPress SEO, speed review, plugin hygiene and maintenance guidance are available. Hosting and paid plugin costs stay with the client.
Will my website be SEO-friendly?
The build can include SEO-ready structure and on-page basics, but a website cannot guarantee organic visibility by itself. Content quality, demand, competition, authority and ongoing maintenance still matter.
Can you redesign only one page?
Yes. A landing page, service page or home page can be scoped as a focused project when that is the highest-priority improvement.
Process, pricing and handover

Questions about working together.

How does the freelance process work?
We start with a brief and fit check, agree the scope and deliverables, confirm access and timing, complete the work, review the handover and decide whether any next phase is useful. The exact steps vary by project.
Are the prices fixed?
The published figures are starting-from references. The final quote depends on scope, number of pages or campaigns, research depth, implementation and revisions. The agreed scope is documented before work begins.
What happens if I do not know which service I need?
Tell me the business problem rather than selecting a service. I can help compare SEO, ads, web development, lead research or a smaller consultation and explain what should come first.
