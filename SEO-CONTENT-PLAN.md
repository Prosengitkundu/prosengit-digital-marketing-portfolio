   improved summaries/objectives; titles kept factual.

   international conversion (timezones, remote process, what to prepare, response promise).
8. **FAQ** — added international-client questions (remote work, contracts, payments,

9. **Blog (existing 21)** — every article: improved excerpt, added internal links to
   services + related articles, added FAQ + natural CTA sections; 6 commercial pillars

    Meta Ads (Facebook/Instagram) guide; Local SEO & Google Business Profile guide;

    H2/H3, steps, mistakes, best practices, FAQ, CTA, internal links. Same categories,
    same rendering system — no structural change.

    `site.js` fallback); CTA paragraphs expanded with service keywords.
12. **Metadata** — unique title + description + OG/Twitter per page; JSON-LD
