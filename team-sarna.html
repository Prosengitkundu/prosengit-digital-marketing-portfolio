NSDA Level 3 Certified Digital Marketer

Sarna Haldar is a results-driven marketing strategist who helps businesses build stronger online identities. She focuses on connecting with the audiences and turning digital opportunities into real growth.

As a NSDA Level 3 Certified Digital Marketer Sarna combines practical knowledge, creative thinking, strategic planning and a strong commitment to learning. Her skills cover marketing, WordPress website design, SEO, Google Ads, Meta Ads, social media marketing, content strategy and audience research.

She believes that every successful digital effort starts with understanding the business and its audience. Whether it’s building a website improving search rankings or launching targeted ads Sarna creates solutions that are clear user-friendly and aligned with what the business wants to achieve.

🚀 Her Professional Approach

Sarna sees marketing as more than just creating content or running ads. For her effective marketing happens when strategy, creativity, technology, audience insights and measurable goals all come together.

Her method follows five steps:

Understand → Strategize → Create → Optimize → Grow

She takes time to learn about each business find out who their customers are analyze the market and competitors and then build a clear plan. From there she puts in place tools and actions that help improve online visibility and support long-term success.

Her attention to detail ability to adapt, attitude and dedication to getting better every day let her bring a high level of professionalism and purpose to every project.
