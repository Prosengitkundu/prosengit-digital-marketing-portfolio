COMMON QUESTIONS

Hiring a freelance specialist, answered

Short answers to the questions international clients ask most. Read the full FAQ →

What does an SEO freelancer do?

A freelance SEO specialist researches your market, audits your website, fixes technical and on-page issues, plans content around keywords your customers actually search, and reports progress in plain language. See SEO services for exact deliverables.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes — I work remotely with businesses in the USA, UK, Canada, Australia, Europe, the Middle East and Asia via email, WhatsApp and video calls scheduled around your timezone. International projects are quoted in USD with written scope and reporting. Start with a free consultation →

Can you manage Google Ads and Meta Ads?

Yes — campaign strategy, audience research, setup, conversion tracking, monitoring and optimization across Google Search, Display, YouTube, Facebook and Instagram. Ad spend is paid by you directly to the platform; your accounts stay under your ownership.

Do you build websites with HTML/CSS and WordPress?

Yes — custom hand-coded HTML/CSS/JavaScript websites are my primary web service (fast, responsive, SEO-friendly), and WordPress development, customization and optimization are available for clients who want a CMS. Compare both in HTML vs WordPress.

How long does SEO usually take?

Technical fixes can show effect within weeks; content-driven gains typically mature over 2–4 months. I never guarantee rankings — instead you get honest timelines, verifiable reporting and fast course-correction.

How does your freelance process work?

You share your goals, I recommend the most practical starting point, and we confirm scope, fixed starting-from price and timeline in writing before anything begins — then work proceeds with review checkpoints. See packages and pricing →
