Before you enquire

Useful answers before a sales call.

Read all FAQs
Can you work with clients outside Bangladesh?
Yes. Projects are available remotely for businesses in international markets. The scope, communication and payment arrangements are agreed before work starts; I do not create fake local offices or locations.
How long does SEO take?
SEO is not a fixed-time result. Technical fixes can be completed quickly, while visibility and qualified enquiries depend on competition, site history, content quality and market demand. I will explain the sequence and measurement plan rather than promise a ranking.
Can you build a website and optimise it for search?
Yes. Custom HTML/CSS/JavaScript and WordPress projects can include information architecture, accessible markup, metadata, internal links, image optimisation, sitemap and analytics-readiness. See the web development service for scope.
What should I include in my first message?
Share your website or business link, target market, what you want to improve, any deadline and the service you are considering. A short, honest brief is more useful than a long list of keywords.
