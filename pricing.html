PRICING FAQ

Common pricing questions

Are these prices final?

All package prices are “starting from” rates. Final pricing depends on project scope, complexity and requirements. You will always receive a written quote with the exact scope before any work begins or any payment is due.

Why do you show prices in both BDT and USD?

I work with clients in Bangladesh and internationally. Clients based in Bangladesh are quoted in Bangladeshi Taka (৳); international clients are quoted in US Dollars ($). The scope of work is identical — only the currency changes.

Does the Paid Advertising price include the ad budget?

No — advertising budget is separate. The service fee covers strategy, audience research, campaign setup, monitoring, optimization and reporting. The ad spend itself is paid by you directly to Google or Meta, so you keep full ownership and transparency of your ad accounts.

Do you build WordPress websites too?

Yes, but it is not my main focus. My primary web development service is custom HTML, CSS and JavaScript development, which gives you cleaner code, better performance control and stronger SEO foundations. WordPress is available as an additional option if you specifically want to manage content with a CMS.

Can I combine services or change a package?

Absolutely. Custom packages are available based on project scope, business goals and requirements. Use the custom quote section below and describe what you need — I will suggest the most practical combination and price it honestly.
