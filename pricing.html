What affects the quote?

Scope is the honest pricing variable.

A five-page website and a thirty-page website are not the same project. A campaign review and monthly optimisation are not the same service. A lead list without an ICP can create more rows but less value. The quote changes when the work changes.

  • Number and complexity of pages, campaigns, markets or records
  • Whether research, copy, design, implementation or training is needed
  • Access to analytics, advertising accounts, CMS, hosting and existing files
  • Revision rounds, approval speed, integrations and required handover
  • Whether the work is one-time, monthly or tied to a defined batch
Common pricing questions

Choose the right scope, not the biggest package.

Can I start with a smaller project?
Yes. An audit, consultation, keyword map, landing page or research batch can clarify what should happen next before a larger engagement is considered.
Are international clients quoted in USD?
The current menu uses BDT for Bangladesh clients and USD as a reference for international clients. The final quote, payment method and any taxes or platform fees are confirmed directly for the project.
Is advertising spend included?
No. Google, Meta or other media spend is paid directly to the platform and remains separate from the service fee.
Do you guarantee SEO results or leads?
No. Ranking, traffic, leads and sales depend on factors outside a freelancer’s complete control. Pricing covers the agreed work and deliverables, not a guaranteed business outcome.
How do I request a custom quote?
Use the contact form and include your website, target market, desired service, current problem and any deadline. I can then confirm fit and define the scope.
