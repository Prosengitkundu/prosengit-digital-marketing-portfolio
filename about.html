PROFESSIONAL BIOGRAPHY

From Khulna to international clients

My full name is Prosengit Kundu Utshob, and I work professionally under the brand Prosengit Kundu. I am based in Khulna, Bangladesh, and I serve clients both in Bangladesh and internationally — across the USA, UK, Canada, Australia, Europe, the Middle East and Asia. My professional identity is simple: I am a Digital Marketing Expert and SEO Specialist first, with deep supporting expertise in custom-coded web development, lead generation, paid advertising, social media marketing, graphic design and professional training.

My journey started with design — learning the craft of visual communication, then teaching it at ARICHO IT in Khulna. Teaching design forced me to understand not just how tools work, but why visual decisions succeed or fail in the real world. That curiosity pulled me deeper into the internet itself: how people find businesses on Google, how websites are actually built, how advertising platforms decide who sees what. Digital marketing became the discipline that connected everything I was learning.

Over the following years I trained and worked across the full digital marketing stack — SEO, keyword research, Google Ads, Meta Ads, social media marketing, lead generation, analytics and web development. Along the way I served as a Digital Marketing Level-3 Trainer at the Technical Training Center in Khulna, and as a Database & Digital Marketing Trainer at the Department of Youth Development, Khulna — a Bangladesh government institution where I helped young people build practical, income-ready digital skills.

Today I work as an independent freelancer and consultant. That means the person who plans your SEO strategy can also hand-code your landing page; the person who builds your lead list also understands how those same audiences behave in ad platforms. Clients get one coherent system instead of three disconnected vendors — and one accountable point of contact who understands the whole picture.

I don't outsource responsibility and I don't hide behind jargon. Every deliverable comes with plain-language explanation, honest timelines and transparent pricing. If something is not worth your money, I will tell you — because my reputation is built on clients who come back and refer.
