What this service is for

The work should answer a business question, not fill a checklist.

Meta can create demand, capture leads and bring warm audiences back to a business, but the platform cannot repair an unclear offer or a weak landing page. I start with the conversion path. We decide what counts as a meaningful action, what information the sales process needs and what the campaign can realistically learn with the available budget. The ad account, creative and landing page are treated as one system.

In practice

What paid-social optimisation means in practice

Paid social improves through a sequence of questions: did the right person see the message, did the creative make the offer clear, did the landing page match the promise and did the business respond appropriately to the enquiry? Looking only at cost per click hides the parts of the system that determine lead quality.

I prefer structured tests over constant random edits. An audience, message or format should have a reason for being tested, a sensible amount of time to learn and a decision rule for what happens next. If the data is too limited to support a conclusion, the report should say that rather than turning a small sample into a performance claim.

The sales handoff matters as much as the campaign. Lead forms can ask for useful qualifying context, thank-you pages can set expectations and a simple follow-up process can prevent good enquiries from being lost. Those details are part of a responsible Meta Ads plan.

For Meta Ads, I need the offer, audience, service area, budget assumptions, creative assets or production constraints, lead-handling process and access to the relevant account or tracking context. If the offer is still being decided, consulting may be the better first scope.

Questions clients usually ask

Before you decide, here is the useful detail.

Do you manage both Facebook and Instagram ads?
Yes. Meta Ads Manager can distribute campaigns across Facebook and Instagram when that placement mix supports the objective. The final placement choice is based on audience, creative and measurement rather than a blanket promise.
Do you provide ad creatives?
I can help plan copy, hooks, formats and creative direction. If a project needs a large volume of video or advanced design production, that scope is agreed separately or coordinated with an appropriate specialist.
Is the ad budget included?
No. Your Meta media budget remains separate and is paid to Meta. My quote covers the agreed strategy, setup, creative direction, optimisation or reporting scope.
Can you fix an existing Meta Ads account?
Yes. An account review can identify campaign structure, tracking, audience overlap, creative fatigue and lead-quality issues before deciding whether to rebuild or improve what already exists.
